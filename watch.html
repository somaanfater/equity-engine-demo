Watch list

Reasons a saved report might be out of date, recomputed every weekday. Four checks run against each ticker's frozen snapshot: a new 10-K, 10-Q or 8-K; a daily price move beyond two standard deviations of its own trailing volatility; an open-market insider purchase; and published distress thresholds being crossed.

Every check is arithmetic. No language model is involved and nothing here is a recommendation to buy or sell: a trigger says the analysis should be looked at again, not what the answer will be.

Checks that could not run are listed under each ticker. "Nothing fired" and "nobody looked" are different, and a watch list that hides the second reads as an all-clear it has not earned. A threshold is also skipped where its source model does not describe the company: Altman (1968) and Beneish (1999) were both estimated on samples that excluded financial firms, so neither is applied to a bank.
