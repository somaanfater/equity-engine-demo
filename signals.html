Do these signals actually work?

Every signal on this site is tested here against what the share price went on to do. No language model is involved: these are the computed columns, measured against realised returns in excess of the S&P 500 over the same window.

Each observation is dated at the first trading day after the 10-K was filed, not at the fiscal year end. A Piotroski score for a September year end is not knowable until the filing lands five weeks later, and dating it earlier would hand the signal five weeks of hindsight.
