Interactive

Screener

Four screens over every covered ticker. Each runs in your browser against the same computed figures the reports use — nothing is recomputed on a server, and nothing is fetched from a vendor.

A screen is a filter, not a finding. These rank the covered set on one dimension each. A name passing a screen has not been analysed — that is what the sixteen AI phases and the council do, and none of them has run on these tickers.
